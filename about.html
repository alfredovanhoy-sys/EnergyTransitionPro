About EnergyTransitionPro

Driving Ireland's renewable energy future through expert corporate finance and strategic advisory.

Our Mission

EnergyTransitionPro was founded with a clear mission: to provide world-class corporate finance advisory services that accelerate Ireland's transition to a sustainable, low-carbon energy system. We believe that the success of the green energy transition depends not only on technological innovation but also on the quality of the financial and strategic frameworks that underpin it. Our team brings together deep expertise in project finance, mergers and acquisitions, financial modelling, and regulatory strategy to support developers, investors, and institutional stakeholders at every stage of the project lifecycle.

Who We Are

We are a specialised advisory firm focused exclusively on the Irish renewables sector. Our team comprises seasoned professionals with extensive experience across investment banking, project finance, infrastructure advisory, and energy policy. We have advised on transactions totaling over €500 million in value and have supported more than 50 renewable energy projects from concept through to financial close and operational handover.

Our clients range from early-stage developers seeking to structure their first project finance facility, to established institutional investors conducting due diligence on portfolio acquisitions. We tailor our services to the specific needs of each engagement, ensuring that our advice is practical, commercially focused, and aligned with the strategic objectives of our clients.

Our Expertise

Project Finance

We have extensive experience structuring non-recourse project finance facilities for wind, solar, and energy storage projects across Ireland. Our services include the preparation of bankable financial models, the development of information memoranda, and direct engagement with lenders and equity investors. We understand the nuances of the Irish market, including the RESS framework, the evolving capacity market, and the requirements of the major lending banks active in the sector.

Mergers & Acquisitions

Our M&A advisory practice supports both buy-side and sell-side transactions in the renewable energy space. We assist clients with portfolio valuations, target identification, deal structuring, and negotiation support. Our deep knowledge of the Irish project pipeline and market dynamics enables us to provide strategic insights that enhance transaction outcomes.

Due Diligence

We conduct comprehensive financial, commercial, and regulatory due diligence for investors and lenders. Our due diligence reports cover project viability, revenue security, construction risk, operational performance, and legal compliance. We identify potential risks early in the process and propose practical mitigations that protect our clients' interests.

Financial Modelling

Our financial models are designed to meet the exacting standards of institutional investors and development finance institutions. We build models from the ground up, incorporating project-specific assumptions, sensitivity analysis, and scenario planning. Our models are transparent, auditable, and designed to facilitate the investment decision-making process.

Strategic Advisory

We provide strategic advisory services to developers and investors seeking to optimise their positioning in the Irish renewables market. This includes market entry strategies, capital structuring advice, regulatory analysis, and growth planning. We help our clients navigate the complex policy landscape and identify opportunities that align with their investment objectives.

Our Values

Integrity: We provide honest, transparent advice that puts our clients' interests first. We believe that long-term success is built on trust and credibility.

Expertise: We invest continuously in our knowledge and capabilities, ensuring that our advice reflects the latest developments in the market, technology, and regulatory environment.

Collaboration: We work closely with our clients and their advisors, fostering a collaborative approach that delivers the best possible outcomes for all stakeholders.

Sustainability: We are committed to supporting the transition to a sustainable energy future. Our work is driven by a genuine belief in the importance of renewable energy for Ireland's economic and environmental well-being.

Why Choose Us

EnergyTransitionPro offers a unique combination of deep sector knowledge, financial expertise, and a client-focused approach. We are not a generalist advisory firm that dabbles in renewables; we are specialists who live and breathe the Irish energy market. Our track record speaks for itself, and our clients return to us project after project because they value the quality and reliability of our advice.

Whether you are a developer seeking to secure project finance, an investor evaluating a potential acquisition, or a corporation looking to secure a corporate PPA, EnergyTransitionPro has the expertise and experience to guide you through the process with confidence and clarity.
